Grown-ups & curious minds

Little games. A little help.

Play without an account

The games save progress in the browser on this device. Favorites are saved here too. There is no player account or cross-device sync in this prototype.

Keep what you make

Browser data can be cleared or lost. DoodleBox can export editable projects. A picture export from Nail Salon saves the artwork, rather than the full game progress.

Moving to another device or browser? Make a moving link for My Aquarium and My Fuzzlet saves.

A place on your Home Screen

Every game has app-like launch support. Open the game directly, then use your browser’s install option or Safari’s Add to Home Screen.

Installation does not automatically mean offline play.

Find your own pace

The motion button turns off the Peekarium’s decorative movement. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected automatically. Each game has its own settings.

Peekarium is an early version. Final privacy policies and support details are being prepared separately.
