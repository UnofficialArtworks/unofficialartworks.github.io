Privacy & support

Your games stay with you.

For kids: your pets, fish, nail designs and doodles live on your own device. We never ask your name, and we can’t see your games.

What we never do

No accounts or sign-ups, no ads, no purchases, no chat, no cookies and no tracking or analytics. Nothing you type or make in a game is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Each game keeps its progress in this browser. My Aquarium and My Fuzzlet keep their saves, including the names children give their tanks and pets. Nail Salon and DoodleBox keep their creations, and DoodleBox keeps a copy of itself so it can open offline. Photos brought into DoodleBox stay on the device. The Peekarium remembers favorites and the motion setting.

Links you choose to send

A moving link or a My Aquarium tank link carries its saves or tank inside the link itself, after the “#”, the part browsers never send to a server. Anyone who has the link can see what it holds, so share it only with people you trust. Pictures you save go to your device.

Who delivers the pages

GitHub Pages hosts Peekarium. Like any web host, it receives your device’s IP address when a page loads, and GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security (see its privacy statement). The pages load nothing from any other company: no outside fonts, scripts or ads.

Clearing or deleting

All the games share one address, peekarium.com, so clearing this site’s data in your browser settings erases every game’s saves at once. Make a moving link first to keep them. On iPhone and iPad, a game added to the Home Screen keeps its own saves, and deleting its icon deletes them.

Questions or problems

Write to hello@peekarium.com. Grown-ups only, please, and leave out children’s photos, full names and other personal details. Messages go to the maker of Peekarium and are used only to reply.
